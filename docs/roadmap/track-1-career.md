## Immigration constraint

STEM OPT requires an **E-Verify enrolled employer** and a Form I-983
training plan with a genuine employer-employee relationship.

1099 gig platforms (Outlier, Mercor, DataAnnotation) do **not** qualify.
The contractor rung is closed.

Unemployment clock: 150 aggregate days across the whole OPT period.
That is the real deadline, not savings.
